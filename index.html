Overview

What is the GDSR?

The Global Data Standards Repository holds the standards that every clinical trial dataset must meet before FDA or EMA submission: CDISC (SDTM and ADaM), eCRF and controlled terminology. Researchers use it daily to look up variables, codelists and reports.

Problem statement

What was happening earlier?

A heuristic audit and workflow shadowing revealed four systemic bottlenecks in the legacy portal. Researchers worked around them with exports and manuals.

Buried navigationUnindexed lab metadataRigid data gridsHidden anonymisation rulesExcel workaroundsPDF SOP lookups

Opportunity

Reorganise navigation around researchers’ mental models so data models and terminologies are reachable in two clicks.

Unify search across variables, lab measurements and value-level metadata, inside accessible, compliant data grids.

Research and discovery

Identifying the real problem

I ran contextual inquiries, heuristic evaluations, open and closed card sorting, and tree testing with 24 clinical data managers and biostatisticians. Restructuring the taxonomy around their mental models cut decision time by 40%.

52% → 94%

First-click success in tree testing (n=24) after rebuilding the navigation taxonomy using Hick’s Law.

    Design decisions and impact

    Design decisions that replaced exports and manuals with one governed flow

    • Promoted controlled terminology to primary navigation with instant in-grid filtering.
    • Unified search across lab parameters and value-level metadata.
    • Surfaced codelist mappings inline with badges and expandable row drawers, not modals.
    • Added contextual GxP anonymisation tooltips and two-click access to reports.
    • Tokenised the interface on the Roche R Design System with full ARIA grid semantics and 4.5:1+ contrast.
